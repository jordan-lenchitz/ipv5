# ipv5 utility

## features
- **ipv5 dashboard**: view your current quantum-entangled dynamic ip
- **mac entanglement**: xor your mac address with ipv5 for "modern" security
- **port predictor**: uses your device's battery percentage to suggest the best open port for whatever
- **boomerang ping**: routes your localhost ping through the moon for maximum accuracy
- **slow-dns resolver**: a dns resolver that takes its time, just like the old days
- **ipv7 mode**: unleash the future of networking with base64 addresses and chaotic ui
- **dns roulette**: randomly resolves domains to other domains in your history
- **quantum packet loss**: packets exist in a superposition of both dropped and delivered until observed
- **blockchain arp**: decentralized arp table that takes 10 minutes to resolve a mac address
- **udp handshake**: forces a 3-way handshake on udp packets just to assert dominance
- **tcp window breaker**: automatically maximizes tcp window size until the router cries
- **http/0.9 downgrade**: strips all headers and formatting for maximum performance
- **bgp roulette**: advertises your local subnet to the global internet (results may vary)
- **icmp scream**: ping packets are 65535 bytes of pure /dev/urandom
- **subnet mask generator**: creates abstract non-contiguous subnet masks for security by obscurity
- **wifi de-authenticator**: kicks yourself off the network to save battery
- **localhost load balancer**: round-robins traffic between 127.0.0.1 and 0.0.0.0
- **ipv4 exhaustion simulator**: randomly drops 30% of your packets to simulate a world without ipv4
- **ipv5 literal**: hardcodes your ip to 5.5.5.5 regardless of your actual network
- **ssl/tls downgrader**: ensures your traffic can be read by nostalgia enthusiasts
- **mac address randomizer**: changes your mac address every 5 milliseconds
- **packet fragmentation maximizer**: splits every packet into 1-byte fragments
- **traceroute visualizer**: generates a literal map of the world but gets the coordinates wrong
- **port knocker**: knocks on ports to the tune of "shave and a haircut"
- **dhcp rejector**: aggressively sends dhcpnack to all devices on the network
- **ping of life**: sends malformed icmp packets that somehow resurrect crashed routers
- **cloud latency injector**: artificially adds 500ms of latency to make on-prem look better
- **ethernet over dns**: tunnels all your traffic via txt records for ultimate stealth
- **bluetooth le web server**: serves a 1kb website via bluetooth low energy broadcasts
- **vpn to null**: securely routes all your traffic to /dev/null
- **NAT transversal**: traverses NAT by physically walking to the router and pressing the reset button
- **syn flood self-defense**: syn floods yourself to build immunity
- **mtu path discovery denier**: silently drops all icmp fragmentation needed packets
- **bgp hijacker**: claims ownership of 8.8.8.8 for 5 seconds every hour
- **wep encryption enforcer**: downgrades wpa3 to wep for vintage vibes
- **ip-over-avian-carriers simulator**: delays packets by exactly 4 hours to simulate rfc 1149
- **tcp keep-alive spammer**: sends a keep-alive every microsecond to show you care
- **proxy chain loop**: routes traffic through 7 proxies that eventually just route back to you
- **dnssec invalidator**: intentionally mangles dnssec signatures to keep things exciting
- **qos minimizer**: gives your traffic the absolute lowest priority on the network
- **snmp public community stringer**: broadcasts your network topology to everyone
- **ipv6 to ipv4 translator**: converts ipv6 back to ipv4 by just throwing away the last 96 bits

## the ipv1-9 protocol specification
we at ipv5 are proud to support the full evolution of networking protocols:

| protocol | name | format | use case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ipv1** | unary smoke signals | `1111.11.1111` | hand-cranked modems and smoke signals |
| **ipv2** | pure binary | `01101.011` | sentient toasters and coin-flip logic |
| **ipv3** | geographical | `123-main-st.apt-4` | physical mail routing (usps fallback) |
| **ipv4** | boring | `192.168.1.1` | normies and legacy hardware |
| **ipv5** | quantum xor | `x.x.x.x.x [flux]` | our current flagship protocol |
| **ipv6** | try-hard | `2001:db8::1` | people who think hex is cool (often realn't) |
| **ipv7** | base64 premium | `atdwb2lksjk=` | 9-octet chaos for premium users |
| **ipv8** | emoji routing | 🍎.🚀.👽.🍕 | gen z networking stacks |
| **ipv9** | sub-ether | `yesterday.3pm` | packets arrive before they are sent |
